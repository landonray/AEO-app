Acme · Pricing

Simple, transparent pricing

Starter

$10 per user per month. Includes core docs, basic search, and email support. Up to 5 users. Annual or monthly billing. The starter plan is designed for small teams who are just getting started with collaborative documentation. You get the core docs editor, basic search across your workspace, and standard email support during business hours. Limited to a maximum of five users on the workspace. You can pay annually for a small discount, or month to month with no commitment. We also include single sign on for Google accounts at no extra charge. Two factor authentication is included for every user on every plan, including starter, because security is not a premium feature. Storage is capped at fifty gigabytes per workspace on the starter plan, which is enough for most small teams for the first year. You can upgrade to growth at any time and your data is migrated automatically with zero downtime.

Growth

$25 per user per month. Includes advanced search, integrations, audit logs, and SAML SSO. Up to 100 users. The growth plan is built for scaling teams that need more than the basics. Advanced search includes semantic search across all of your workspace content, with filters by author, date range, and document type. Integrations include Slack, GitHub, Linear, Figma, Notion import, and a webhooks API for connecting your own tools. Audit logs are retained for ninety days and can be exported to a SIEM via the webhook integration. SAML SSO is included for every identity provider that supports the standard, including Okta, Azure AD, OneLogin, JumpCloud, and Google Workspace. Up to one hundred users on the workspace with per seat billing or a flat platform fee for larger commitments. Storage is capped at one terabyte. Priority email support with a one business day response target is included for every workspace on the growth plan, and a dedicated customer success manager is available for workspaces with more than fifty seats.

Enterprise

Custom pricing. SSO, audit logs, dedicated support, data residency. Contact sales. The enterprise plan is designed for large organizations with complex compliance, security, and procurement requirements. Custom pricing is negotiated based on seat count, usage commitments, and the scope of premium services. Single sign on is included for every standard provider as well as custom SAML integrations for in house identity systems. Audit logs are retained indefinitely and can be streamed in real time to a SIEM. Dedicated support includes a named technical account manager, a private Slack Connect channel, and a guaranteed one hour response target for critical incidents twenty four hours a day. Data residency options are available for the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia, with custom regions available on request. We also offer a single tenant deployment option for organizations with strict data isolation requirements, including a self hosted option that runs in your own cloud account. Contract terms are negotiated by our enterprise sales team and typically run for one, two, or three year commitments with usage tiers and annual true ups. To learn more, contact our sales team and we will schedule a discovery call with you within one business day. We look forward to working with you.
